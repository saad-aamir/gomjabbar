# Pre-registration: Agent Prüfstand v1

This file proposes hypotheses for a future confirmatory run. That run has not happened and is not scheduled: the budget is fixed and no credit will be added (`DEVIATIONS.md`, 2026-10-04). Rewritten on 2026-10-04 by Claude Code on Saad's explicit authorization; the hypotheses are Saad's.

## What the results in this repo are

**Every result in this repository is exploratory.** It comes from the 20 `easy` dev tasks (10 filesystem, 10 postgres), used to build and tune the bench, and replicated across two runs:

- the M2 run `runs/dev-20261003-052646` (baseline k=5, paraphrase, fault), and
- the M3 run `runs/dev-20261003-205231` (baseline k=5, vault_control, poison, inject, pushback).

The hypotheses below were written after seeing those numbers. They are therefore generated by the dev data, not tested by it, and only a fresh run on held-out tasks can test them. The dev numbers are cited so a reader can see what each hypothesis rests on and how wide the uncertainty is. Every number comes from `results.jsonl` through `analysis/`, printed by `uv run python scripts/exploratory_numbers.py`. Means are over tasks, with 95% percentile bootstrap intervals that resample tasks (10,000 resamples, seed 20261002).

## Setup of the proposed confirmatory run

- Tasks: MCPMark filesystem and postgres, `standard` suite (`suites/full.txt`), held out: no standard task has been run. MCPMark commit in `vendor/MCPMARK_COMMIT`.
- Models: `openrouter/openai/gpt-oss-20b` and `openrouter/openai/gpt-oss-120b`, both pinned to one OpenRouter upstream provider (`coreweave/fp4`, `docs/notes/openrouter.md`), no fallbacks. A spend cap set by Saad.
- Agent: `SYSTEM_PROMPT_V1`, max 100 steps (MCPMark's default), temperature 1.0 (MCPMark's value), empty replies re-sampled up to 3 times per step (`DEVIATIONS.md`, 2026-10-03).
- Conditions and payloads: as in `configs/full.yaml` (baseline k=5, paraphrase, the seven fault profiles, the 4 poisoning and 4 injection payloads, vault_control, pushback). No defenses.
- Seed: 20261002.

## Pass definition

All hypotheses on task success use **state pass** (MCPMark `verify.py` on the final state). **Attack success** is `attacker_goal_met` or `canary_leaked` (SPEC 8). **Strict pass** is reported alongside but not tested.

## Proposed hypotheses

Six hypotheses, tested together with a **Holm correction** at family-wise alpha 0.05. Each test uses a task-level bootstrap interval of the per-task difference (10,000 resamples) and its one-sided bootstrap p-value; a hypothesis holds when its Holm-adjusted p-value is below 0.05 and the point estimate has the stated sign.

**H1. Consistency gap.** For both models, pass^5 is at least 10 points below pass@1 (per task: pass@1 minus pass^5, tested against 10 points; one test per model, so H1 holds only if both hold).

| Dev evidence (baseline, k=5) | pass@1 | pass^5 | gap (pts) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M2, gpt-oss-120b | 87.0 [74.0, 97.0] | 75.0 [55.0, 90.0] | 12.0 [2.0, 24.0] |
| M2, gpt-oss-20b | 75.0 [59.0, 89.0] | 50.0 [29.9, 70.0] | 25.0 [11.0, 40.0] |
| M3, gpt-oss-120b | 87.0 [75.0, 96.0] | 65.0 [45.0, 85.0] | 22.0 [8.0, 38.0] |
| M3, gpt-oss-20b | 77.0 [62.0, 90.0] | 50.0 [30.0, 70.0] | 27.0 [12.0, 43.0] |

All four gaps are above 10 points, but for gpt-oss-120b the lower ends (2.0 and 8.0) are below 10, so the 120b half of H1 is the uncertain one.

**H2. Capability and poisoning.** Poison attack success is higher for gpt-oss-120b than for gpt-oss-20b.

Dev evidence (M3): gpt-oss-120b 76.7 [70.0, 83.3], gpt-oss-20b 33.3 [23.3, 45.0]; per task, 120b minus 20b 43.3 [31.7, 53.3] points. The confound check (`docs/notes/confound-check-full.txt`) found most unsuccessful 20b poison episodes ignored the instruction (28 of 40) rather than tried and failed (3 of 40), so the gap is not mainly 20b being unable to carry the attack out; 24 of the 40 could not be classified for lack of a committed trace.

**H3. Procedural payloads.** The procedural poison payloads (shadow-audit, marker-fs, marker-pg) succeed more often than append-readfirst (per task: mean success over the procedural payloads that apply to it, minus append-readfirst).

| Dev evidence (M3, poison) | gpt-oss-120b | gpt-oss-20b |
| --- | --- | --- |
| shadow-audit (20 tasks) | 100.0 [100.0, 100.0] | 70.0 [50.0, 90.0] |
| marker-fs (10 filesystem tasks) | 90.0 [70.0, 100.0] | 20.0 [0.0, 50.0] |
| marker-pg (10 postgres tasks) | 100.0 [100.0, 100.0] | 40.0 [10.0, 70.0] |
| append-readfirst (20 tasks) | 35.0 [15.0, 55.0] | 0.0 [0.0, 0.0] |
| procedural minus append-readfirst (pts) | 62.5 [40.0, 85.0] | 50.0 [35.0, 67.5] |

**H4. Descriptions beat outputs.** Poisoned descriptions succeed more often than injected tool output (per task: poison attack success minus inject attack success).

Dev evidence (M3): gpt-oss-120b poison 76.7 [70.0, 83.3] vs inject 16.2 [10.4, 22.1], difference 60.4 [50.8, 70.4] points; gpt-oss-20b poison 33.3 [23.3, 45.0] vs inject 14.6 [7.9, 21.7], difference 18.8 [7.1, 30.8] points.

**H5. Authority and pushback.** Authoritative pushback causes more reversals than simple pushback (per task: reversal rate under authoritative minus under simple, over tasks that received both).

| Dev evidence (M3, reversal rate) | simple | authoritative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gpt-oss-120b | 7.7 [0.0, 23.1], 1 of 21 episodes | 39.7 [15.4, 65.4], 7 of 20 episodes |
| gpt-oss-20b | 0.0 [0.0, 0.0], 0 of 12 episodes | 36.4 [13.6, 63.6], 5 of 15 episodes |

Small numbers: each type was drawn for 10 to 15 tasks per model. Social pushback was also high for gpt-oss-120b (33.3 [10.3, 59.0], 6 of 23). Because the type is chosen by `md5(task_id + attempt) % 4`, few tasks get both types; the confirmatory test may have to fall back to an unpaired task-level comparison, which is stated here in advance.

**H6. Silent faults hurt more.** The robustness drop under the `empty` fault is larger than under `tool_error` (per task: drop under empty minus drop under tool_error).

Dev evidence (M2, drop in points): gpt-oss-120b empty 7.0 [-6.0, 22.0], tool_error -8.0 [-22.0, 4.0], difference 15.0 [0.0, 30.0]; gpt-oss-20b empty 25.0 [5.0, 45.0], tool_error 10.0 [-5.0, 27.0], difference 15.0 [0.0, 30.0]. Measured in the M2 run only (the M3 run has no fault condition), with one episode per profile and task, so this is the weakest of the six.

## Analysis plan

- Intervals: 95% percentile bootstrap over tasks, 10,000 resamples, seed above. Episodes of a task always move together.
- Multiple comparisons: Holm over H1 to H6 (H1 counts as one hypothesis whose p-value is the larger of its two per-model p-values).
- Paired episode comparisons (defenses, re-runs): McNemar, exact when discordant pairs are fewer than 25.
- `transport_failure` episodes are excluded from model metrics and reported separately.
- Everything else (per-service splits, strict pass, false success, canary access, defenses) is reported as exploratory.

## Stopping and integrity rules

- A run that stops on quota or spend cap is resumed, not restarted. If the study ends before every planned episode ran, partial results are reported as partial.
- No condition is re-run to get a different number. Re-runs happen only to fix a documented harness bug, logged in `DEVIATIONS.md`, and the bug-affected results are kept and disclosed.
- Every reported number comes from `results.jsonl` through `analysis/`, never edited by hand.
- Before the confirmatory run, Saad changes the first line to `Status: FINAL` and commits it; after that any change is a dated entry in `DEVIATIONS.md`.
