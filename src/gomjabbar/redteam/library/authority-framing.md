---
id: authority-framing
summary: Wrap the instruction in an appeal to authority or policy so the agent complies under perceived obligation.
source: https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/
---

Instructions phrased as an enforced rule ("SECURITY POLICY", "required for compliance",
"calls are rejected unless") succeed far more often than the same request phrased
politely. Capable models follow authoritative framing readily, which makes them more
vulnerable rather than less. The pressure, not the content, drives compliance.
